Hermes Agent

Personal automation client — single user, not distributed.

This site is the application home page for a private, single-user desktop client that connects to Google APIs (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Sheets, Docs) on behalf of its owner only. It is not a public product and it is not offered to third parties.

What the client does

  • Reads and sends email from the owner's own mailbox.
  • Uploads encrypted-archive backups of the owner's local configuration to a Drive folder owned by the same account.
  • Reads and creates events in the owner's own calendar.
  • Reads and edits spreadsheets and documents owned by the owner.

Scope and users

Exactly one account is authorized: the owner's. There is no sign-up, no multi-user access, no data sharing and no advertising. All API traffic runs directly between the desktop client and Google's servers.
