Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Hermes Agent is a private desktop client operated by a single person for their own use. This policy describes what it does with Google user data.

1. Who controls the data

The owner of the Google account that authorizes the client. No company, no third party and no other user has access to it.

2. What is accessed

  • Gmail — messages in the owner's own mailbox, read and sent on the owner's explicit instruction.
  • Google Drive — a folder named “Hermes Backups” owned by the same account, used to store configuration backups.
  • Google Calendar — the owner's own calendars.
  • Google Sheets and Docs — files created by or shared with the owner.

3. Where data goes

Nowhere else. All processing happens locally on the owner's computer. Data is exchanged only with Google's own API endpoints. Nothing is sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising or profiling. No analytics or telemetry is sent anywhere.

4. Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored locally in the client's configuration directory. Backups are stored in the owner's own Drive folder and on the owner's own disk; the client keeps the three most recent archives and deletes older ones. Removing the client, or revoking its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, ends all access immediately.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page. No request to the developer is required.
